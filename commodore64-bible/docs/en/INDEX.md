# Welcome

**Every journey needs a starting point.**

This is yours.

The Commodore 64 Bible has been designed as a network of Knowledge Trails rather than a traditional book.

There is no single path through the project.

However, if this is your first visit, the following reading order is recommended.

# After the Foundation

Once you have completed the Foundation documents, you are ready to choose your own Knowledge Trail.

Whether you want to understand the hardware, learn Assembly language, study the VIC-II, master the SID, reverse engineer commercial games or explore demo scene techniques...

...there is a Trail waiting for you.
