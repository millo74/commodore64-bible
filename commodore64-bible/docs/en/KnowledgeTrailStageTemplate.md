# Purpose

This template defines the standard structure used by every Knowledge Trail Stage.

Its objective is to provide a consistent learning experience throughout the Commodore 64 Bible.

Every Stage should guide the reader through discovery, understanding, experimentation and reflection.

A Stage is not simply a lesson.

It is one step along the Knowledge Trail.

## The Story Begins

Introduce the topic using simple language.

Avoid assuming previous technical knowledge.

Build curiosity before introducing technical details.

Whenever possible, begin with a real-world analogy or an everyday observation.

---

## Technical Exploration

Present the technical explanation.

Progress from simple concepts to more advanced ones.

Define every new technical term before using it extensively.

Whenever possible, include diagrams, examples and illustrations.

---

## Engineering Insight

Explain why the hardware or software was designed this way.

Describe the engineering decisions behind the implementation.

Whenever possible, discuss trade-offs and historical constraints.

---

## Laboratory

Invite the reader to perform one or more practical experiments.

Learning is reinforced through observation.

Every experiment should have a clear objective.

---

## Historical Stop

Describe how programmers of the Commodore 64 era approached this topic.

Include historical anecdotes whenever appropriate.

---

## Vista Point

Pause.

Connect the concepts learned in this Stage with previous Stages.

Help the reader build a mental model.

Do not introduce new concepts here.

---

## Summary

Briefly review the most important ideas.

The reader should be able to remember the key concepts after reading this section.

---

## Suggested Next Stage

Indicate the next recommended Stage.

If alternative learning paths exist, explain them.
