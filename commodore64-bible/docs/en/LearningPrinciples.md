# Purpose

The Commodore 64 Bible is not only a technical reference.

It is a learning journey.

These principles define how every document, every Knowledge Trail and every Stage should teach.

They ensure that the project remains coherent, accessible and enjoyable, regardless of its future size or the number of contributors.

---

# Principle 1 -- Curiosity Before Knowledge

Every Stage should begin with curiosity.

Start with a question.

A story.

A surprising fact.

Or a real-world problem.

People remember discoveries better than definitions.

---

# Principle 2 -- Never Assume

Never assume previous knowledge.

Every technical term should be introduced before being used.

Every concept should build naturally upon the previous one.

---

# Principle 3 -- The Layer Principle

Every important concept should be explained through four complementary layers.

## ? Layer 1 -- Intuition

Explain the idea using simple language, analogies or everyday experiences.

The goal is understanding.

Not precision.

---

## ? Layer 2 -- Practical View

Describe what actually happens.

Focus on observable behaviour.

Help the reader connect theory with reality.

---

## ? Layer 3 -- Technical View

Present the hardware, software or assembly-level explanation.

Registers.

Memory.

Signals.

Instructions.

Data structures.

---

## ? Layer 4 -- Engineering View

Explain why the system was designed this way.

Discuss historical constraints, trade-offs and optimization strategies.

Whenever possible, relate the topic to commercial games, demos and real-world programming techniques.

---

# Principle 4 -- Why Should I Care?

Every Stage should answer an important question:

**Why is this worth learning?**

Motivation should never be left to the reader.

---

# Principle 5 -- Observe Before Explaining

Whenever possible:

observe first;

explain later.

Understanding grows naturally from observation.

---

# Principle 6 -- Experiment Before Memorizing

Knowledge becomes permanent through experimentation.

Whenever possible, encourage the reader to verify every concept personally.

---

# Principle 7 -- Build Mental Models

Avoid teaching isolated facts.

Help the reader build a coherent mental model of the Commodore 64.

Every new concept should connect with previous knowledge.

---

# Principle 8 -- Connect Everything

Knowledge is a network.

Not a sequence of isolated documents.

Every Stage should reference:

* prerequisites;
* related concepts;
* future applications.

Learning becomes stronger when connections become visible.

---

# Principle 9 -- Respect History

The Commodore 64 represents decades of engineering creativity.

Historical context is part of technical understanding.

Whenever possible, explain how programmers of the 1980s and 1990s approached the same problem.

---

# Principle 10 -- Teach Like a Mentor

Do not write like a manual.

Do not write like an encyclopedia.

Write like a mentor walking beside the reader.

Guide.

Observe.

Encourage.

Explain.

Inspire.

The goal is not only to transfer knowledge.

The goal is to create new explorers.

---

# Final Thought

The Commodore 64 Bible is not a collection of answers.

It is a place where curiosity becomes understanding.
